Highlights from Ballparks of America’s five-day Prep Kits and their Registered Teams page. These are the Summer 2026 editions; the 2027 kits are “coming soon,” so days and times may shift.

Road to Ballparks

  1. By late Feb · 5+ months out Lock the roster, book travel Coach adds the roster in EventConnect, and each family gets an email to fill in their player’s profile, sizes and waivers. Book family lodging and flights or the drive.
  2. By late Apr · 3+ months out Custom jerseys and hats The team designs its tournament jerseys and hats with Triton. They’re waiting in the team suite at check-in, and players keep them.
  3. By Jun 29 · 30 days out Trading cards and add-ons Order trading cards and upload player photos (the deadline is 30 days before). Order bedding, photos, shirts, Family Feast and theme park tickets.
  4. Early to mid Jul · 1–4 weeks out Plan the week The game schedule comes out about a week before. Plan Branson time around it: Silver Dollar City, the lake, dinners.

🏨 Family lodging

  • Families must book through EventConnect, the official housing partner (required for teams traveling more than 75 miles).
  • Families on the team have to book at least 8 rooms every night of the tournament, or the team pays a fee of up to $1,000.
  • Players and coaches stay in the team suites on campus.
  • Team favorites: Element Hotel right next door (breakfast, pool, kitchens), the Cabins at Grand Mountain across from campus (sleep 6–10, good for two families), condos on the Thousand Hills golf course, or Table Rock Lake resorts about 15 minutes away.

✈️ Getting there

  • Most teams fly into Springfield-Branson (SGF), 45–50 minutes from campus, with rental cars on site. Direct flights from hubs like Chicago, Atlanta, Charlotte and Dallas.
  • The small Branson Airport (BKG) is only 22 minutes away, if you can find a flight.
  • Backup: St. Louis (STL), more direct flights from the East Coast, then about a 3-hour drive.
  • Driving: about 1,200 miles. Parking and admission are free for players, coaches, families and fans.

📱 Stay connected

  • Get the Ballparks of America – EC app for schedules and scores.
  • Weather delays and updates come by text and email from EventConnect, so keep your contact info current.
  • The schedule posts 7 days before and isn’t final until the night before game one.
  • Every game streams live on HiCast Sports for family back home.

🧢 Packing for players

  • Uniform, belt, socks, hats, glove, bat, cup, catcher’s gear
  • Molded cleats only (no metal), turf shoes for pitching
  • Twin XL sheets, blanket and pillow (or rent them)
  • Towel, shower shoes, toiletries, sunscreen, bug spray, meds
  • Swim trunks, beach towel, laundry bag and detergent
  • Blitzball and wiffle ball gear for the late-night games

💳 Money on campus

  • The campus is cashless. Cash is only taken at the Pro Shop, where you can also trade it for a gift card.
  • A preloaded campus gift card is the easiest way for players to buy food and fun. You can set limits and reload it.
  • Extras you can add through EventConnect: bedding, trading cards, photos, shirts, family event meals, Silver Dollar City and White Water tickets, gear rentals.
  • Custom MLB-style trading cards come in packs of 32, 64, 96 or 128. Players trade them all week.

🚫 Campus rules

  • No gum or sunflower seeds anywhere on campus. It can get a player, coach or fan ejected.
  • No outside alcohol (coolers are fine), no smoking or vaping, no grills.
  • No bikes, scooters, skateboards or Heelys.
  • Leashed pets are OK outdoors, but not on the fields.
  • Players must always be with at least one other player.

🎢 Branson fun

  • Players already get a multi-day White Water pass and a packet of discount coupons for area attractions. Discount Silver Dollar City and White Water tickets for families are in EventConnect.
  • The Ballparks attractions portal has discount tickets for shows, tours, tubing and more.
  • Tournament-team discount on Table Rock Lake pontoons through RockLane Marina.
  • Pin trading is a thing here: bring team pins (Ballparks’ partner is BJ’s Trophy, 1-800-951-7822).

⚾ The week

  • Day 1: check-in, Media Day, Welcome Dinner, trading card swap, then Opening Ceremonies at St. Louis Stadium. Families arrive around 7:45pm.
  • Skills competitions and a Players (and Moms!) Home Run Derby
  • Pool play, then bracket play and the championship on the last day
  • Field lights stay on until about midnight for pickup games
