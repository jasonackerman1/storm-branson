— Road to Ballparks
Four phases. One unforgettable week.
From the day your deposit lands to the day you check in — here’s the timeline most teams follow.
— Phase 01 · 5+ Months Out
Lock the roster, book the travel
Enter your roster of players and coaches in EventConnect. Parents and Coaches complete the surveys and upload player information. Book family lodging through EventConnect and confirm flight or drive plans.
— Phase 02 · 3+ MONTHS OUT
Design you custom hats and jerseys with Triton
Every team at a weeklong tournament gets custom designed jerseys and hats including in the tournament package. We partner with Triton to handle the design, production, and delivery.
Jerseys and hats are waiting in the team suite Saturday afternoon when you arrive. Players take them home.
— Phase 03 · 2+ Months Out
Trading Cards and Add-ons
Trading Cards
. Choose your designs, place your trading card orders, and upload your player images for Iconix to start the manufacturing process.
Add-ons.
Review the Add-ons section of your Team Dashboard and place your orders for bedding, photography, weekly rentals, tournament shirts, and other items.
— Phase 04 · 2-4 Weeks Out
Plan your week
As your game block schedule is released (showing your day off and time blocks during the week you will have games) it's time to plan your week outside of baseball. Purchase your Silver Dollar City tickets, book your boat rentals, schedule tee times, and plan your Branson activities.
Ballparks Guest Services is here to help answer your questions and provide suggestions.
Need a full packing list?
Sample schedule PDFs and a printable team packing list are inside EventConnect under your team’s dashboard. Call (417) 464-6333 if anything’s unclear.
— EventConnect
Your team dashboard. Schedules. Hotels. Payments.
Everything operational lives in EventConnect — your registration platform. Sign in to your team account to manage rosters, view schedules, book housing, and pay balances.
Team Login on EventConnect
— Step 01
Register your team
Pick your tournament, lock in your age group, and reserve your spot with the $1,000 deposit. Confirmation email goes to the head coach. Your EventConnect team dashboard opens up the moment registration clears.
— Step 02
Make your roster & invite the team
Add players inside your dashboard. EventConnect emails each family directly — they fill in profiles, sizes, and emergency contacts on their own. Coaches don’t chase down forms.
— Step 03
Book Family Lodging
Review the EventConnect lodging options and determine where your families are going to stay. You can create a hotel room block for entire team, reserve a couple 6-10 bedroom lodges or, book individual condos on the golf course or elsewhere. Choose the best experience for your team.
— Step 04
Trading Cards and Add-ons
Order your customer trading cards, photography packages, Family Feast tickets, theme-park tickets, and team merch — all from the same dashboard,.

— Roster
Invite the team. Families do the rest.
Coaches don’t chase down forms. Add each family’s email and EventConnect sends parents a direct invite to enroll their player. Once everyone’s in, you’re ready for the next step.
— Step 01
Coach builds the invite list
Open the Roster tab in your EventConnect dashboard. Add a row for each player — name and a parent’s email is all you need to send the invite.
— Step 02
EventConnect emails each family
Parents get an email with a secure enrollment link. One email per player. Re-send from the dashboard if anyone misses it.
— Step 03
Parents enroll their player
Each family fills in the player profile — date of birth, jersey number, position, sizes, emergency contact, and waivers — directly through the link. Coach sees the green checkmark as each one comes in.
— Step 04
Move on to the next step
Once every player is enrolled, the roster’s ready. Coach gets a confirmation email and the dashboard advances you to accommodations.

MLB-style cards. Your kid’s face on the front.
Professional design. Real photo. Your player’s name, position, season stats. Players trade them all week. Families keep them forever.

— Travel
Fly into an airport less than an hour away.
SGF is 45 minutes from campus. STL is the major-hub backup. Most teams fly in, pick up a rental, and they’re parking at the field by lunch.
— Closest Airport · 45 min from campus
SGF · Springfield-Branson National
The everyday choice. Direct flights from Dallas, Atlanta, Denver, Chicago, Houston, Charlotte, Nashville. Rental cars on-site. 45-minute drive south on US-65 to the campus.
— Major Hub · 3 hr 15 min from campus
STL · St. Louis Lambert International
The hub option if SGF connections don’t work. More direct flights from coast cities. ~3-hour drive south to Branson.

— FAQ
Questions teams ask the week before.
The fast answers. For anything not covered, the hospitality line is (417) 464-6333.
When will my Ballparks of America tournament schedule be available?
Tournament schedules will be available approximately one week before the tournament via your EventConnect team portal. Schedules are subject to change, so please check the website or app daily during the tournament.
What is the maximum number of players allowed on a roster at Ballparks of America?
The player roster will be limited to 14 players and 4 coaches. If teams have additional players or coaches, exceptions may be made on a case-by-case basis, but note – Team Suites have a maximum of 18 beds and 14 lockers. Teams can have 4 coaches and one additional scorekeeper in the dugout.
How do the families book hotels for Ballparks of America tournaments? Are discounts available?
Booking through EventConnect is required for teams and groups traveling from more than 75 miles away from Ballparks of America. The traveling distance is determined by the submitted address at the time of registration.
For weeklong tournaments: Each team is responsible to book a minimum of 8 hotel room nights by families and friends per each night of the tournament – which is 48 total room nights per team. Any teams that do not meet the minimum booked hotel rooms are subject to an additional $1,200 fee due by April 1, 2025.
For 4-day tournaments: Each team is responsible to book a minimum of 8 hotel room nights by families and friends per each night of the tournament – which is 24 total room nights per team. Any teams that do not meet the minimum booked hotel rooms are subject to an additional $1,000 fee due prior to team check-in.
Once your team registers for an event at Ballparks of America, you will automatically receive lodging information from EventConnect.
What is the Ballparks of America refund policy if my team cancels?
The initial $1,000 deposit is non-refundable. Subsequent payments are non-refundable after the due date has passed.
Team registration payment schedule: $1000 due upon registration $3000 due November 30, 2024 $3000 due January 30, 2025 Remaining balance due April 1, 2025
Are families allowed to bring food and coolers to Ballparks of America?
Outside coolers are allowed on campus. No outside alcohol is permitted.
When can my team check-in to our Team Suite at Ballparks of America?
Teams may check into their team suite starting at 3:00 PM on Saturday of their tournament week.
What should players bring when they are staying in the Team Suites at Ballparks of America?
A packing list in the Tournament Prep Kit for your tournament. Visit your EventConnect team portal or our Know Before You Go page.
Does Ballparks of America have a dress code for the coaches?
Coaches are expected to dress appropriately. They should wear either team or Ballparks of America apparel. It is preferred but not required that coaches match during Opening Ceremonies and Championship Day.
Are pets welcome on the Ballparks of America campus?
Friendly pets are welcome at Ballparks of America. All pets must be leashed at all times, well-behaved, and cleaned up after. Pets are not allowed on the fields. BoA management reserves the right to remove a pet and their owner at any time.
Is there pin trading among teams at Ballparks of America?
Yes, there is pin trading among teams. Teams are encouraged to come ready to trade by bringing their own pins or ordering them in advance from our partner BJ's Trophy. Visit their website bjstrophy.com or call 1-800-951-7822.
What is the typical schedule like for a weeklong tournament at Ballparks of America?
Saturday - Check in begins at 3 pm; Opening Ceremonies at 8 p.m.
Sunday - Skills Competitions; Pool Play begins
Monday & Tuesday - Pool Play
Wednesday - Pool Play; Home Run Derby Championship
Thursday - Off Day! Teams can enjoy Branson!
Friday - Single Elimination Bracket Play; Championship Game
*Itinerary is subject to change*
